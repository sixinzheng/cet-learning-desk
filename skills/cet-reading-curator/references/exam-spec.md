# Exam structure and six-level calibration

Primary references:

- National Education Examinations Authority syllabus: https://cet.neea.edu.cn/res/Home/1704/55b02330ac17274664f06d9d3db8249d.pdf
- CET-4 public test structure: https://cet.neea.edu.cn/html1/report/16123/196-1.htm

Do not store past papers in the skill. Extract only structure, cognitive demand, timing implications, and short compliant examples.

## Formats

### Banked cloze

- 10 numbered blanks and 15 candidates.
- Candidates are unique; each correct candidate is used once.
- Test contextual meaning and grammatical fit. A distractor must be plausible in part of speech but wrong in context.
- The website's production task belongs to the vocabulary module, not the six-topic careful-reading inventory.

### Long matching

- A multi-section text with labelled sections and 10 statements.
- A section may support more than one statement when evidence genuinely does so.
- Statements paraphrase rather than copy the evidence. Each statement has one best section.

### Careful reading

- One coherent passage and exactly five A-D questions.
- Across a set, cover main idea, detail, inference, contextual word meaning, and attitude/purpose. A single passage should cover at least four of these.
- Item order broadly follows the passage except for global main-idea or purpose items.
- Correct answers are paraphrases. Distractors use relevant language but fail through scope, relation, attribution, certainty, or relevance.

## Difficulty levels

| Level | Site label | Reading demand |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | CET-4 pass transition | 180-230 words; mostly direct information; at most one light inference |
| 2 | CET-4 ordinary transition | 210-260 words; familiar compound sentences; one inference |
| 3 | CET-4 strong baseline | 240-300 words; common CET-4 long sentences and paraphrase; one or two inferences |
| 4 | CET-6 entry | 270-330 words; cross-sentence reference and implicit contrast; one or two inferences |
| 5 | CET-6 baseline | 300-370 words; denser argument and abstract nouns; two inferences |
| 6 | CET-6 strong | 330-420 words; high information density, qualification and evaluation; two or three inferences |

Length is only one signal. Also assess vocabulary band, clause depth, reference distance, information density, abstraction, distractor closeness, and the proportion of cross-sentence reasoning.

## Adaptation

Keep the factual spine and remove names, quotations, incidental chronology, local detail, and rhetorical repetition that do not serve the learning goal. Rebuild the passage around one question or tension. Never change a number's meaning, turn correlation into causation, or invent a speaker. If a fact cannot be checked from the source brief, omit it.
